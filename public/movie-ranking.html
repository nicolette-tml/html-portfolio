The Best Movies According to Nicolette

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Iron Man

Best Marvel superhero origin story of all time. Robert Downey Jr. embodied Tony Stark's character so well.

Wicked: For Good

As the sequel to Wicked (2024), we follow more of Glinda's growth in this second movie as she becomes more mature and realistic about the cruel world around her.

Spider-Man: Brand New Day

I just watched it yesterday (15 August) so I might be biased. But it really captured the essence of Peter Parker's friendly neighborhood Spider-Man vibe so well like the first and second Spider-Man movies.
